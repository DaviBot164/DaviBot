# Casual social scope and reply-purpose shortening

## Collected cases

- Jealous or nah, shortened friendship clarification involving Davi/Daviti, he helped me that's it, wasn't tryna make you mad, and modal easy-question requests with so.
- Negated insults paired with answer confusion, reporting someone else's insult with wasn't me, defending the bot, defending Daviti, and returning to normal chat.
- Sarcastic answer criticism, requests to explain without drama, an explicit apology/reset, and a new polite question.
- Game preference recall followed by shortening: You prefer PvP, rather than a new definition of PvP. Repeated shortening retains that purpose. Shortening a definition still yields a definition.

## Scope and memory

Social shorthand is recognized with whole-utterance patterns. No global n-to-and or pronoun rewriting is used, and learned phrase keys remain unchanged. A general jealousy question no longer names Davi or establishes a Davi antecedent. If he helped me has no introduced target, the reply asks who instead of guessing. Davi/Daviti friendship wording can explicitly establish that target.

Answer criticism preserves an existing preset explanation reference and its semantic answer kind. Explanation uses that reference when present; unknown answers still require clarification. Report and defence metadata distinguish speech reports, negation and the person being discussed. Explicit reset clears the earlier conversation context.

## Verification and limits

The new offline suite covers every collected chain, each persona, held-out seed/case/polite variants, unknown pronouns, subject and negation controls, semantic metadata, explanation-reference preservation, reset and reply-purpose checks. The prior social-depth assertion after game recall now expects a preference summary rather than a definition, correcting the meaning identified in live testing.

No new slash schema, database changes, external AI calls, XP or moderation changes. Existing isolated lab and send-path tests remain in the full runner. This is bounded recognition of supported forms, not universal English understanding or a general accuracy score.
