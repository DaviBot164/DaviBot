- How u been, curly-apostrophe I'm well, no I'm just tired, u have progress, nice mommy with trailing comma-addressed kit, goodnigh and byee.
- Moonlight and silence, adding stars, waves with a cool breeze, a crowd restriction, a scene suggestion, and a request for just one simple detail.

- Mae's requested directed nickname Davi, retaining the existing friend tone and ID-based owner distinction.

## Design

Daily wording is recognized as bounded whole utterances; the change does not use general fuzzy matching. The ordinary English noun kit stays protected. The special trailing-kit form requires the clear comma-delimited nice/good mommy wording; quoted/reported occurrences remain protected. Nickname responses still use actual account IDs.

Help is represented by a topic, helper key/label and stage rather than being gated on Davi's name. Known helpers Ash/Mae, a/my friend and someone are supported; a clear capitalized proper-name form is also bounded to a short alphabetic name. This is a conversational label, not an account identity or permission. Corrections preserve the help subject and replace the helper. Known pronoun constraints remain checked; neutral they is accepted.

Scene contributions accept bounded known descriptors composed with and. Semantic detail enums, no-crowd constraints and a suggestion key are retained. A simple/one-detail request shortens the same suggestion. Other authors, channels and reset topics cannot borrow these slots.

Mae's configured recipient name is rendered at the final text stage only for her real account ID. Her tone remains mae; owner checks, learned keys, Davi/Daviti person aliases and Ash's voice are unchanged. Two old exact-pool assertions now expect the requested nickname rendering while retaining full pool coverage and tone checks.

## Verification and limits

The new suite exercises all collected chains, curly punctuation, modal/case/seed variants, owner/member/friend persona isolation, Mae-as-Davi versus actual-owner nickname privileges, helper correction/recall, scene detail retention, negatives, noun/quoted address protection and context reset. Existing dialogue, send-path, learning, rollback and cooldown suites remain included.

No new paid service, external AI, database schema or slash command changes. Named labels and scene descriptors are bounded supported forms; this is not arbitrary English comprehension or a general accuracy measurement. Open-ended descriptions outside the supported vocabulary may still need clarification.
