# Independent actions, evidence and editable scenes

## Reviewed cases

- Helped me out; with understanding the rules; past confusion but current understanding.
- Mae explained the rules while Ash sent the link, both as a compound message and separate statements.
- Who explained the rules, who sent the link and who helped me, with independent answers.
- No breeze, just waves; current scene-detail recall; a few people are fine; rejecting a lighthouse and requesting a different detail.

## Behavior and state

Help has distinct helper, explainer and link-sender roles. Recording an action does not overwrite another role or invent an unreported role. Unknown-role recall explicitly says the user has not supplied it. Explicit named clauses record their authors. Pronoun explanations use the introduced actor and retain known pronoun constraints. Helper corrections update the current referent without rewriting independent action facts.

Naming the server-rules topic no longer claims an explanation occurred or that the user understood. Past confusion/current understanding are explicit semantic flags. Explanation facts require an explicit statement. Gratitude remains neutral when several people contributed.

Scene editing can remove a known detail or retain only a stated replacement. Recall lists current details, not removed ones. A few people is compatible with avoiding a busy crowd; no people and crowds allowed are distinct constraints. Rejected suggestion keys are excluded from subsequent suggestions. One-detail requests retain the current accepted suggestion. If a finite pool is exhausted, the bot asks for direction rather than reviving rejected details. An explicit re-add can restore a previously removed detail.

State contains bounded semantic slots only and is committed by the existing successful-send path. Author/channel scope, expiry and topic reset remain in force. Mae's Davi nickname, owner-only voice, learning, moderation and progression are preserved.

## Validation and limits

The new offline suite covers every collected chain, missing-role recall, separate-role updates, explicit understanding evidence, scene removal/recall, constraint clarification and relaxation, repeated rejections, re-addition, resets, isolation, polarity/subject negatives, personas, seed/case variants and non-mutating rendering. Existing suites remain unchanged except for registering the new suite.

These are supported bounded English forms, not arbitrary-language comprehension or a general accuracy score. Action names and scene vocabularies outside the supported grammar can still require clarification. No external AI, database schema, paid service or slash command change is added.
